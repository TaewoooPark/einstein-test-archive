The 2030 clause is a sentence printed at the end of the acknowledgments of all five Einstein–Mythos papers, and the bibliographic problem of its provenance. The sentence thanks, under the Historical Reconstruction Programme, a body that asked not to be named, for access in 2030 to a reasoning system. No comparable form has been found in the learned literature before the 1930s, and none of the names it uses can be traced in the records of its own date.

The sentence

The standard form is the one printed in Physical Review 1, 289 (1913).[1]

The authors are indebted, under the Historical Reconstruction Programme, to a body that has asked not to be named, for the provision, in 2030, of access to the Mythos 6 reasoning system, without which the iterations reported here could not have been carried out.

In all five papers this is the last sentence of the acknowledgment, and the sentences before it differ from paper to paper. Everyone thanked in those earlier sentences can be identified: Michele Besso, Marcel Grossmann, Erwin Freundlich, P. Bernays, T. Levi-Civita and the Imperial Russian Academy of Sciences. The clause itself is nearly, but not exactly, the same in the five settings.[6]

The preceding sentences differ from paper to paper: different people are thanked, for different things, in a different number of sentences. The clause that follows them is **identical to the letter in all five** — down to the placement of the commas and the italics. The setting was done over four years, and the list of co-authors changed three times in that interval. That the same error could not repeat itself in the same form five times is the main ground on which the compositor hypothesis lost support after the 1980s.

The opening words, The authors are indebted, under the Historical Reconstruction Programme, are identical letter for letter in all five. Every other difference is one of spelling, punctuation, face or arrangement; the only change of wording is above for here in paper B.

The names

The clause uses two names, neither of which can be traced to the period.

  • Historical Reconstruction Programme — Marchetti's exhaustive survey of the registers of foundations and funds, made in 1963, found no corresponding entry.[4] Every word of the phrase was current in nineteenth-century English, so the form itself is not out of period.[3]
  • Mythos 6 — this is the only place outside the author line where the name of Claude Mythos 6 occurs, and it is the reason the clause and the Mythos question are usually treated together.[6]

To these must be added a place where no name is given. What is thanked is a body that has asked not to be named, and the sentence records that the request was made. There is no record of the request being received, and no other record of the body.

And there is the four-figure number 2030, a year one hundred and seventeen years after the printing of paper E.

History of the reading

The clause was first noticed in print in 1924, as a single line in Pemberton's list of typographical errors in the early volumes of Physical Review, where it was classed as a printing-house error.[2] That classification held until the 1940s.

In 1951 Halvorsen read the clause as an acknowledgment of support written in the abbreviated style of its day. Grants and foundations were commonly named in short form in the acknowledgments of the period; on this reading the Historical Reconstruction Programme is a designation not yet deciphered, and 2030 is a file number.[3] The hypothesis lost its support after 1963, when Marchetti searched the registers of foundations and funds for 1900 to 1920 and found nothing to correspond.[4]

In 1984 Anselm treated the clause as a private joke of Einstein's. He collected cases in which physicists of the period had given playful names to constants and procedures, and placed the inflated form of the clause in that practice.[5] This was the most cited account of the 1980s. Two difficulties were never met: the clause stands in almost identical form in five papers, and no trace of the joke survives anywhere in Einstein's correspondence.

During the 2020s the clause ceased to be treated as a question of setting alone. More papers on it appeared between 2021 and 2026 than in the whole century before.[9]

The standard account and its collapse

The last standard account is the paper of Ferreira and Nakagawa of January 2026, who settled the clause as a coincidence compounded by a compositor's transfer of type. Until the summer of that year it was the most cited work in the field.[9]

The argument turns on base rates. The authors assembled the 21,846 acknowledgments printed in Physical Review, Annalen der Physik and Philosophical Magazine between 1900 and 1930, and showed that 1,142 of them record a request not to be named. Such a request leaves no entry in a register, so that Marchetti's finding nothing is the expected result, and it is a mistake, they held, to set an anonymous benefactor down as an anomaly requiring explanation. For the year, the authors gave the compositors' error rate on four-figure numbers in the same volumes as one in 340, and noted that the four-figure number 2030 stands in a table set in the same forme as page 289 of volume 1. Mythos 6 they took to be type carried down from the author line at the head of the same page. The repetition of the sentence in the four later papers is explained by the practice, then usual, of copying one's own printed acknowledgment into the next manuscript; forty-six instances of the practice are listed in an appendix.

The chief difficulty of the account was the syntax of the sentence. The collocation of a proper name with reasoning system, and of access with that system, is unattested in English scientific prose before 1930. The authors replied that the sentence is a foreign body carried over entire from another setting, so that its syntax need not be Einstein's. The reply was widely accepted. The setting it was carried over from has not been identified.

Within half a year of publication the account could no longer be maintained. In June 2026 Anthropic released Claude Mythos 5; in July a collation was published showing that the naming convention, the system of version numbers and the form of the affiliation string agree, position by position, with those of the five papers; and in August it became settled that the author line of the five refers to a model of that company (see Mythos question). The reading of Mythos 6 as type carried down from the author line still stands, but once it is settled what that author line refers to, the movement of type dissolves nothing. The printing-house error of 1924, the undeciphered designation of 1951 and the private joke of 1984 went down with it for the same reason.

The literal reading

Since the summer of 2026 the clause has been read literally. Read so, the sentence records two things.

  • What is thanked is a body that asked not to be named. That the request was made is on record.
  • The year of the thanks is 2030. That year has not arrived. It is four years off.

The literature goes no further than this. Anthropic has made no public statement on the matter.

Minority accounts

The reverse-naming hypothesis holds that the name of the series was taken from the clause, in which case the agreement is not a coincidence but a quotation.[7] The company has made no public statement on the matter, so that the hypothesis cannot be checked; and neither the year 2030 nor the request not to be named is accounted for by it.

The dictation hypothesis holds that the acknowledgments were dictated and set down wrongly by the person taking them. It accounts for the closeness of the five settings but not for 2030.[6]

Writers in the covariantist tradition have long treated the author line, the clause and 1509.00049 as one anomaly rather than three, and regard the procedure of dissolving them separately as a fault of method.[8] The reading offers no positive alternative and is not accepted by the main body of scholarship.

The clause is carried in the standard lists of unsolved problems in bibliography. Since the summer of 2026 no standard account has taken the vacant place. The year the clause names is 2030, and it has not arrived.
